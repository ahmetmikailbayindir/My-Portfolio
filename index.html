01 / The Brief

Technician foundation, full-stack builder

My foundation is hands-on. At Kelesoglu IT & Security Systems I repaired and imaged machines, installed CCTV and access control, ran structured cabling, and troubleshot LAN, WAN, and IP-CCTV issues on site. That is where I learned that reliable systems are built one careful, documented step at a time.

Since then I have moved up the stack. As a freelance Web Developer and Cloud Infrastructure contractor I stood up AWS EC2 with Nginx and an Apache reverse proxy, Docker on Ubuntu, and real AWS VPC networking (subnets, NAT and internet gateways, route tables). At The Home Store I ran a Shopify storefront end to end with Square POS, hosting, domain, and advertising.

Today, at CognaWorks Inc., I build software across data analytics, embedded systems, and mobile. The combination is the point: a systems and networking foundation, plus the proven ability to build and ship real systems end to end.
